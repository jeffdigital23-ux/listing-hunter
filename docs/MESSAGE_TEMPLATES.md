# Approved Message Templates

These templates are intentionally short and polite because Jeff is contacting cold owner databases and had a WhatsApp block before.

## Condo owner

```text
Hi, Jeff here from Showcase Properties S/B. I focus on Puchong properties. Just checking if your unit at [Property Name] is available for rent/sale, or should I mark it as not available? If you prefer not to receive messages, just reply No. Thanks.
```

## Commercial owner

```text
Hi, Jeff here from Showcase Properties S/B. I focus on commercial properties around Puchong. May I know if your unit/shoplot at [Property Name] is available for rent or sale, or should I update my record as not available? If not relevant, just reply No and I won't disturb again. Thanks.
```

## Industrial owner

```text
Hi, Jeff here from Showcase Properties S/B. I focus on Puchong industrial properties. Just checking if your factory/warehouse at [Property Name] is available for rent/sale, or if you may consider in future? If not relevant, just reply No and I won't disturb again. Thanks.
```

## Mudah owner check

```text
Hi, Jeff here from Showcase Properties S/B. I focus on Puchong properties. May I know if you are the owner for [Listing Title], and whether you are open for agent assistance to rent/sell it? Thanks.
```

## Safety rules

- Do not auto-send these messages.
- Do not send again if the owner replies `No`, `Not interested`, `Wrong number`, or asks not to be contacted.
- Avoid sending the same template too fast to too many contacts.
- Start lower than 30/day if WhatsApp has recently restricted the number.
